# Visual golden — achievements grid, dark mode

**Image:** `docs/media/02-dark.png` (running app, iPhone 17, dark appearance).

## Intent
- The same content and layout as the light grid, with **adaptive** surfaces: a dark background, dark
  section strips, and light title/value text — all legible.
- The **teal bar** and the **white title** are unchanged (brand-consistent across appearances).
- Badges render on the dark surface without a broken/opaque box; the **Marathon stays ghosted** and
  readable.
- No truncation; contrast remains comfortable in dark mode.
