# Visual golden — the locked Marathon cell

**Image:** `docs/media/01-light.png` (inspect the sixth Personal Records cell — Marathon).

## Intent (the locked-medal rendering — ADR-0007)
- The Marathon badge is visibly **ghosted**: desaturated (no color) and dimmed relative to the earned
  shield badges around it.
- Its value line reads the localized **"Not Yet"** (not a duration).
- It is clearly distinguishable from the earned medals (which are full-color) — this is what makes the
  "5 of 6" count legible at a glance.
- The badge is not a broken/missing image; it is the earned asset with the ghost treatment applied.
